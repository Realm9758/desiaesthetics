## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js 15 static export (React 19, plain CSS, no UI libraries). Chosen by the user to match their four existing clinic sites (DermaMuse, Couture, MG, ASA): all site copy lives in `content/site.ts`, and `npm run export` produces a static `out/` any host can serve. Deploy target: GitHub repo `Realm9758/desiaesthetics`.

## Users

Women, broadly 30–55, living in Petts Wood, Orpington, Chislehurst and the wider Bromley borough, who want injectable aesthetic treatment close to home rather than a trip into central London.

Their situation: most arrive cautious. They are weighing whether to have treatment at all, and their dominant fear is looking obviously "done" or being upsold by a clinic that treats them as a transaction. They are researching on a phone, often in the evening, and they are comparing a handful of local options at once.

Their job: decide whether this specific practitioner is someone they trust to put a needle in their face, and if so, get an appointment booked with the least possible friction.

## Product Purpose

Dessi Stones Aesthetics has no website. Its entire online presence is a Google Business Profile with no site link, no photos and one review. The site exists to become the clinic's front door: to establish that this is a real, careful, appointment-only clinic in Petts Wood, present what it treats in plain language, and move the visitor to a phone call or WhatsApp message.

Success is a measurable rise in calls and WhatsApp enquiries from people who arrived already reassured, and a listing that no longer reads as incomplete.

## Positioning

A single practitioner's treatment room in Petts Wood village, not a chain and not a medspa counter. The visitor sees the same person for the consultation, the treatment and the follow-up. That continuity — one pair of hands, one face, one phone number that is answered by the person doing the treatment — is the claim a larger clinic in Bromley or Orpington cannot truthfully copy.

## Operating Context

- Premises: 215–217A Petts Wood Rd, Petts Wood, Orpington BR5 1LA. Petts Wood is a commuter village; the parade sits close to Petts Wood rail station.
- Enquiry and booking run entirely through one mobile number, 07438 399754, by call and WhatsApp. There is no online booking system, no booking software and no enquiry form today.
- Appointment-only. The Google listing shows the clinic opening at 10am on Mondays.
- Visitors overwhelmingly arrive on a phone, frequently straight from the Google Maps listing.

## Capabilities and Constraints

**Confirmed treatment scope:** injectables and skin boosters — anti-wrinkle injections, dermal filler, polynucleotides and skin-booster style treatments. Nothing else is confirmed; the clinic is not known to offer facials, laser, IPL, or brow and lash services, and the site must not imply it does.

**Regulatory constraint (binding):** UK law prohibits advertising prescription-only medicines to the public. The brand name of the anti-wrinkle injectable must never appear anywhere on the site — not in body copy, headings, alt text, meta description, structured data, image filenames, or inside a quoted review. Generic descriptions ("anti-wrinkle injections", "wrinkle-relaxing treatment") are the only permitted form. Dermal filler and skin boosters are devices rather than POMs and may be described generically.

**Explicitly undecided — must not be invented:**
- Full opening hours. Only "opens 10am Monday" is known.
- All prices. No price, "from" price, or price range may appear until supplied.
- Practitioner qualifications, registration, insurer and prescriber status.
- The exact treatment list, product names and treatment durations.
- Consultation, aftercare, deposit and cancellation policy.
- Social media handles. None were findable.
- The text of the single Google review.

## Brand Commitments

- Trading name: Dessi Stones Aesthetics. "Dessi" is the practitioner's first name and carries the clinic — the personal name is the brand.
- No existing logo, colour palette, typeface or visual identity of any kind. The identity is created here.
- **Standing preference — the category standard.** Offered a rolled conceptual direction (an Ordnance Survey sheet of Petts Wood) alongside three alternates and the category standard, the user chose the standard. This is the second clinic site where they have done so, after DermaMuse. Treat the aesthetics-clinic convention as the committed direction for clinic work unless they say otherwise: execute it at full craft, without irony and without smuggled quirk, rather than proposing an invented visual world.
- **Craft bar: Augustinus Bader, Dr. Barbara Sturm, Aesop.** The build is held to that level of restraint and finish, not to "good for a local clinic".
- **Type-led, no photography.** The user chose to execute the convention in its purely typographic register rather than with generated, placeholder or stock imagery. No human faces, no result images, no stock skin photography.

## Evidence on Hand

- Google Business Profile: category "Skin care clinic", rated 5.0 from 1 review, address and mobile number as above, no website, no photos, no posts.
- **No photography exists.** No clinic interior, no practitioner portrait, no treatment or result images, no logo files. The site must be designed to be genuinely finished without photographs rather than displaying gaps where images should be.
- **No testimonials in hand.** The one Google review's text was not available. No review, quote or client comment may be written or paraphrased into the site.
- Web research returned no Instagram, Facebook, Fresha, Treatwell or directory presence for this business. Absence of a social profile is a confirmed finding, not a gap to fill with a guess.

## Product Principles

1. **Only verifiable claims.** Every factual statement traces to the Google listing or to the owner's own confirmation. Where a fact is missing — a price, an hour, a credential — the site says nothing rather than something plausible.
2. **Reassurance before persuasion.** The visitor's blocking emotion is fear of looking overdone and fear of being sold to. Calm, restraint and specificity convert here; urgency and hard-sell repel.
3. **The phone is the product.** With no booking system, every screen's job is to make calling or messaging 07438 399754 the obvious, effortless next move, especially one-handed on a phone.
4. **No photography is a design constraint, not a deficiency.** The work must look intentional and complete on typography, colour, material and structure alone.
5. **One practitioner, stated plainly.** Scale is not the pitch. Continuity and care are.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No client-specific requirement was established. Baseline: WCAG 2.2 AA contrast, full keyboard operability, visible focus, respect for reduced-motion preferences, and tap targets sized for one-handed phone use, which is the dominant context.
